## What this lesson builds

Dying has to be **productive**. A death screen that just says GAME OVER wastes the
most attentive twenty seconds the player will ever give you.

Show them what the run *was*: depth reached, damage dealt, items collected,
essence banked. The summary is what turns a loss into a reason to go again.
