## What this lesson builds

Once the systems work, layer feel over **everything**: a full-screen FX shader
(`shaders/screen_fx.gdshader`, UV-only so it compiles headless) for the corruption
vignette, low-health pulse and hurt flash; spark streaks and afterimages on attacks;
squash and stretch on stops, dashes and hits; a directional camera kick and
zoom-punch on heavy hits; a wave-clear slow-mo flourish; loot that bursts and
magnets to you; cards that fly in and flash on select.

**Juice is not a step — it's a coat of paint you apply to every interaction.**

Then export, and it ships.
